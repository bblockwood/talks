Already have a talk open on another device?

Every device that enters the same code joins the same talk and stays in step, and each one chooses what it shows. Put the slides on the room's computer and keep your notes on a laptop, or drive from your phone. The slides reach a joining device encrypted. A talk published ahead of time opens with its code here too. On a shared computer, use a private or InPrivate window.
